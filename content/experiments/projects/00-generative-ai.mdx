This category gathers my ongoing experiments with generative AI, where my background in Cinema and Audiovisual meets my practice as a creative technologist. I test the limits and possibilities of generative AI for conveying stories that weren't yet told, trying to uncover the specific potential of AI as a tool.

I approach each piece the same way I would approach a shoot: before generating anything, I draft the story of the scene, plan the camera movement and the cuts, and think through the acting, from the weight of a look to the rhythm of a gesture, until every detail works together the way it should. The AI becomes the set, but the direction stays mine.

These studies are born from my love for witchcraft and the symbols that surround it. Crowns, fire, cauldrons, ritual makeup and the feminine figure as a source of power keep returning as a visual vocabulary I continue to investigate, extending the questions I started asking in earlier works like Reimagining Goddesses and Untold. Each scene is a test of how much intention can survive translation into a prompt, and of how far a cinematic eye can push these models beyond their default aesthetic.

Over time, I have built experience across a range of generative tools, including Higgsfield, FLORA, Krea, Luma and others.
