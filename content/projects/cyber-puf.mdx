## Overview

Cyber Puf was one of three satirical products launched by Smooth Technology as part of A Fool's Errand, released on April Fool's Day 2025 so that no one could quite tell whether they were real. Presented as the world's first AI vape companion, Cyber Puf is a smart case that wraps around your vape, learns your "vapestyle," and supports you through your quitting journey with a calculated mix of encouragement and maybe even a little bit of shame. The website and commercial went live that morning, followed by an Instagram campaign and a launch party in the evening, where the fully packaged product was revealed alongside a vape lounge for one last hurrah.

## Process

Partnering with Jen Vance as Product, Web and Social Media Producers, I worked across almost every layer of the product's identity. I designed the packaging and built the entire website from scratch, translating the humor of the concept into the language of a sleek wellness-tech brand. For the commercial, I took the script and broke it down into a scene-by-scene film plan, organizing what needed to be shot and how each moment would land.

<Pair a="/projects/cyber-puf/assets/website-cards.jpg" b="/projects/cyber-puf/assets/website-colors.jpg" aspect="16/10" />

I also led the product photography from start to finish: developing the concept and moodboard, designing the lighting for each scene, shooting, and editing the final images. Some of the final assets were enhanced and edited with generative AI, pushing the dreamy, pastel world of the brand even further. Together, these pieces created a campaign convincing enough to make people stop and wonder if and where they could actually buy Cyber Puf.
