## Overview

What if every student left an event with a gift that could only belong to them? This was the question the MediaLab at Fundação Itaú brought to THECODE when they invited us to create the app for Trampos do Futuro, a career event for young people. It was the kind of invitation we love, when the question is still open and there is room to imagine new ideas, test different paths and build solutions from scratch.

<Figure src="/projects/trampos-do-futuro/assets/identidade.jpg" aspect="1396/1690" />

Throughout the event, students rated each activity they experienced. These ratings gradually shaped a personalized profile that, at the end of the journey, turned into three digital gifts: a Match Profissional, presenting each student's RIASEC profile in a way that is easy to understand; a unique generative badge, built from the combination of elements in each person's journey; and future career paths aligned with what each student enjoyed the most. AI became part of the process by crossing the ratings with other data from the journey, so that every result was unique and memorable.

## Process

I led the concept, creative direction and project management, guiding my team at THECODE from the visual identity all the way to deployment. The creative challenge was to speak the language of young people without becoming dated, so we created a youthful and timeless identity designed to carry through future editions of the event, drawing on the futuristic influence of vaporwave and 3D elements.

<Figure src="/projects/trampos-do-futuro/assets/avaliacao.jpg" aspect="1396/1287" />

I named the Match Profissional and led the creative thinking behind how the app talks to its audience, turning the whole experience into a gamified journey full of animation and written in the voice of the people who were there.
