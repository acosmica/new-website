## Overview

Trampos do Futuro is an app powered by AI that reads students' reviews of their own experiences and turns them into personalized, tailored assessments of who they might become professionally. What if every student left an event with a gift that could only belong to them? This was the question the MediaLab at Fundação Itaú brought to thecode when they invited us to create the app for Trampos do Futuro, a career event for young people. It was the kind of invitation we love, when the question is still open and there is room to imagine new ideas, test different paths and build solutions from scratch.

<Figure src="/projects/trampos-do-futuro/assets/faces.jpg" aspect="16/9" />

Throughout the event, students rated each activity they took part in, choosing from five reactions that range from "not for me" to "this is so me." An AI then analyzes these ratings and compares them with the weight each activity carries across the RIASEC spectrum: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising and Conventional. This calculation generates a personalized profile of each student's professional personality, a title for their aspiration accompanied by a description of the areas and kinds of work they might be drawn to, based on the inclinations and interests they showed throughout the event.

<Figure src="/projects/trampos-do-futuro/assets/results.jpg" aspect="16/9" />

With this same data, the app generates a unique algorithmic badge, shaped by elements such as how many activities each student attended and their favorite type of activity, so that no two journeys produce the same emblem. Finally, it suggests professions that already exist and others that don't exist yet, opening a window into the careers of the future.

## Process

I led the concept, creative direction and project management, guiding my team at thecode from the visual identity all the way to deployment. The creative challenge was to speak the language of young people without becoming dated, so we created a youthful and timeless identity designed to carry through future editions of the event. Drawing on the futuristic influence of vaporwave, we built a world of floating 3D objects, perspective grids and halftone textures, set against deep blues and lit by bursts of orange, yellow, pink and green.

I named the Match Profissional, the professional match at the heart of the experience, and led the creative thinking behind how the app talks to its audience. The whole experience became a gamified journey full of animation and written in the voice of the people who were there, designed to adapt across phones, tablets and kiosks throughout the event.
